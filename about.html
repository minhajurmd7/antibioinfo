📖 Project Documentation & Academic Overview

Comprehensive technical specification, research background, and architectural documentation for the AB|Info digital health platform.

📄 1. Project Title & Abstract

EVALUATION OF QR CODE-BASED ANTIBIOTICS MEDICATION INFORMATION SYSTEM AND PATIENT ACCEPTANCE AT COMMUNITY PHARMACY

Abstract: Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) constitutes one of the foremost global public health emergencies. AB|Info is an evidence-based digital health intervention and open-access electronic Patient Information Leaflet (e-PIL) system designed to bridge the communication gap between pharmacists and patients in community retail settings. By leveraging quick response (QR) codes printed on dispensing strips, patients gain immediate access to localized, multilingual (English, Hindi, Kannada) dosage instructions, adverse effect warnings, and stewardship reminders.
