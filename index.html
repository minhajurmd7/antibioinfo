Safety & Antibiotic Stewardship ⚠️

✅ Finish the full course

Stop taking antibiotics only when your physician instructs — early discontinuation selects for resistant mutant bacteria.

🚫 Never self-medicate

Never take leftover antibiotics or share with others. Antibiotics have zero activity against viral colds, flu, or viral sore throats.

🦠 Fight superbugs

Misuse and over-the-counter dispensing without culture sensitivity are primary drivers of multi-drug resistance in India.

🔴 The 'Medicines with a Red Line' Campaign

In India, antibiotics and potent prescription medicines feature a prominent vertical red line on their packaging strip. This indicates that the medicine falls under Schedule H or Schedule H1 of the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules and must NEVER be consumed without a doctor's prescription.
