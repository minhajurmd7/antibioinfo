🩺 Do I Really Need an Antibiotic?

Antibiotics kill bacteria, not viruses. Follow this interactive clinical decision guide to evaluate common symptoms and understand when an infection typically requires antibiotics or supportive home care.

🦠 Viral vs. Bacterial

Common colds, influenza, COVID-19, and bronchitis are caused by viruses. Antibiotics have zero activity against viruses and will not relieve symptoms or shorten recovery.

⏱️ The "Watchful Waiting" Principle

Mild ear pain or sinus pressure often resolves on its own within 5 to 7 days with adequate rest, hydration, and fever relief without needing antibiotic exposure.

🚨 When to See a Doctor Immediately

Seek urgent medical attention if you experience difficulty breathing, high fever lasting more than 3 days, stiff neck, confusion, or spreading skin redness.
