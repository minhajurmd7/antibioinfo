💡 1. Myth vs. Fact: Antibiotic Truths

Hover or tap any card to flip it and reveal the pharmacological reality.

❌

Myth 1

"Antibiotics cure common colds, flu, and viral sore throats."

Tap to flip ↻
✅

Fact 1

Antibiotics only kill bacteria. Viruses cause colds, flu, and COVID-19. Antibiotics have zero effect on viruses and cause harmful side effects.

❌

Myth 2

"I can stop taking my antibiotic as soon as my fever or pain goes away."

Tap to flip ↻
✅

Fact 2

Stopping early allows partially resistant mutant bacteria to survive, multiply, and cause a more severe, harder-to-treat recurrence.

❌

Myth 3

"My body becomes immune to antibiotics."

Tap to flip ↻
✅

Fact 3

Your body does not become resistant — the bacteria mutate and become resistant. Resistant bacteria can then spread to family and the community.

❌

Myth 4

"Leftover antibiotics can be saved and reused for my next fever."

Tap to flip ↻
✅

Fact 4

Different bugs require completely different antibiotics. Taking an incorrect drug or partial course breeds resistance and delays proper care.

❌

Myth 5

"Stronger, costlier antibiotics are always better for fast recovery."

Tap to flip ↻
✅

Fact 5

Narrow-spectrum Access antibiotics are best for everyday infections. Using ultra-broad reserve antibiotics wipes out protective gut flora and triggers superbug selection.

❌

Myth 6

"Antibiotics are harmless like vitamin pills."

Tap to flip ↻
✅

Fact 6

Antibiotics carry risks of severe allergic reactions (anaphylaxis), kidney damage, tendon rupture, and life-threatening C. difficile colitis.

❌

Myth 7

"Red line medicines can be bought over-the-counter without a prescription."

Tap to flip ↻
✅

Fact 7

Under India's Drugs and Cosmetics Rules (Schedule H/H1), selling red-line medicines without a valid prescription is an illegal offense.

❌

Myth 8

"If my family member has the same symptom, they can take my antibiotic."

Tap to flip ↻
✅

Fact 8

Dosage depends on age, weight, renal function, allergies, and pregnancy status. Sharing prescription drugs is clinically hazardous.

❌

Myth 9

"Scientists will always discover new antibiotics before old ones fail."

Tap to flip ↻
✅

Fact 9

The global antibiotic discovery pipeline has slowed drastically for decades. Bacteria are developing resistance faster than new molecules are being approved.

❌

Myth 10

"Missing a dose doesn't matter as long as I remember later."

Tap to flip ↻
✅

Fact 10

Antibiotics require steady blood levels above the Minimum Inhibitory Concentration (MIC). Missing doses gives bacteria time to adapt and resist.
