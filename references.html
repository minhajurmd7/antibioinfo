📚 Academic References & Scientific Sources

Peer-reviewed literature, statutory government notifications, and international clinical guidelines underpinning the AB|Info knowledge base.

🏛️ 1. National Guidelines & Regulatory Frameworks (India)

Statutory & National Policy
  • NEWEST • 2024
    ICMR Antimicrobial Resistance Research & Surveillance Network (AMRSN): Annual Report 2024 (8th Edition). Indian Council of Medical Research, Department of Health Research, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, New Delhi.
    • Key Findings: Multi-center longitudinal susceptibility data across 30+ tertiary centers (2017–2024); highlights >70% carbapenem resistance in ICU Acinetobacter baumannii and >90% fluoroquinolone resistance in Salmonella Typhi.
    📄 Access Official ICMR 2024 Report (PDF) ↗
  • NATIONAL STRATEGY • 2025–2029
    National Action Plan on Antimicrobial Resistance 2.0 (NAP-AMR 2.0: 2025–2029): Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) & National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC), Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Nirman Bhawan, New Delhi.
    • Key Mandate: National strategic framework prioritizing community retail pharmacy stewardship, digital patient information systems (e-PIL), and One Health surveillance integration.
    📄 Access Official NAP-AMR 2.0 Blueprint (PDF) ↗
  • ICMR Antimicrobial Resistance Surveillance & Research Network (AMRSN): Annual Report (2023). Indian Council of Medical Research, New Delhi.
  • Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW): Gazette Notification GSR 588(E) dated 30th August 2013 (Insertion of Schedule H1 under Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945). Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO).
    • Statutory Rule: Prohibits over-the-counter dispensing of 3rd/4th generation cephalosporins, carbapenems, and fluoroquinolones; requires pharmacies to maintain a separate register detailing patient, doctor, and quantity for 3 years.
  • MoHFW "Medicines with the Red Line" Campaign: National Awareness Guidelines on Rational Antibiotic Use and Restriction of Over-the-Counter Dispensing (February 2016). Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Govt. of India.
  • National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC): National Treatment Guidelines for Antimicrobial Use in Infectious Diseases (2016). Directorate General of Health Services, Govt. of India.

🌐 2. World Health Organization (WHO) Guidance

Global Standards
  • WHO AWaRe Classification of Antibiotics for Evaluation and Monitoring of Use: 2023/2024 Update. World Health Organization, Geneva.
    • Benchmark: Categorizes molecules into Access (first/second-line with low resistance risk), Watch (higher resistance potential), and Reserve (last-resort). Targets that ≥60% of national consumption should be Access antibiotics.
    🌐 View WHO AWaRe Portal ↗
  • WHO Model List of Essential Medicines (EML): 23rd List (2023). Geneva: World Health Organization.
  • Global Antimicrobial Resistance and Use Surveillance System (GLASS): Report 2022. World Health Organization, Geneva.
  • WHO Global Action Plan on Antimicrobial Resistance (GAP-AMR): World Health Assembly Resolution WHA68.7 (2015). World Health Organization, Geneva.

🔬 3. Local Surveillance & Wastewater Studies (Karnataka & Bengaluru)

Bengaluru, Karnataka
  • Hyperlocal Wastewater-Based Epidemiology for AMR: Longitudinal metagenomic analysis of urban catchment areas in Bengaluru (2022–2023).
    • Key Finding: Detected pan-drug resistant bacteria and elevated metallo-beta-lactamase genes (NDM-1, OXA-48) in approximately 12% of environmental isolates across municipal sewage networks in Bengaluru.
  • Prevalence of NDM-1 and OXA-48 Carbapenemase-Producing Isolates: Tertiary Intensive Care Units across South India (Journal of Global Antimicrobial Resistance, 2021).
  • Karnataka State Action Plan on Antimicrobial Resistance (SAP-AMR Karnataka): Department of Health and Family Welfare Services, Government of Karnataka, Bengaluru.

📖 4. Clinical Pharmacology & Infectious Disease Reference Textbooks

Standard Compendia
  • Essentials of Medical Pharmacology: 8th Edition. Tripathi KD. Jaypee Brothers Medical Publishers, New Delhi, India.
    • Standard reference in Indian pharmacy and medical colleges for antimicrobial mechanisms, food chelation interactions, and adverse event profiles.
  • Goodman & Gilman's: The Pharmacological Basis of Therapeutics (14th Edition). Brunton LL, Hilal-Dandan R, Knollmann BC (Eds.). McGraw-Hill Medical, 2022.
  • The Sanford Guide to Antimicrobial Therapy: 53rd Edition. Gilbert DN, Chambers HF, Saag MS, et al. (Eds.). Antimicrobial Therapy, Inc., 2023.
  • Mandell, Douglas, and Bennett's: Principles and Practice of Infectious Diseases (9th Edition). Bennett JE, Dolin R, Blaser MJ (Eds.). Elsevier, 2020.
