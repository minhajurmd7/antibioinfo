🏛️ National Action Plan (NAP-AMR 2.0)

India's multi-sectoral roadmap (2025–2029) emphasizing retail community pharmacy stewardship, banning non-therapeutic animal growth promoters, and expanding One Health surveillance.

🧪 The One Health Dimension

AMR connects humans, veterinary medicine, and urban water bodies. Metagenomic analysis in Bengaluru demonstrates that antibiotic runoff selects for resistant plasmids (NDM-1, OXA-48) in sewage.

🛡️ What Pharmacists & Patients Must Do

Strictly enforce prescription-only dispensing under Schedule H/H1, never sell partial loose courses without a prescription, and educate patients to complete full treatment durations.
